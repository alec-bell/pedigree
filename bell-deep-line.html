HOW TO READ THIS A solid green line means the link is documented. Amber means probable — one document says so, and nothing contradicts it. Dashed purple means a hypothesis: a candidate, not yet tied in. Red dotted is THE MISSING DOCUMENT — the one gap that would settle the whole line. Grey dashed is a lead: a pointer worth following, nothing more. A red box is a person or branch a document has ruled out.

Everything below William Rasor Bell is your proven line; everything above him is candidate territory, graded honestly.
